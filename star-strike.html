STAR STRIKE

An original VR space shooter — unlimited retries

Open this page in your Quest Browser, then tap ENTER VR below.

You are rookie pilot Jax Vega in the interceptor Kestrel, holding the line against the Void drone swarm.

Left thumbstick steers  ·  Right trigger fires  ·  Left trigger / B = barrel roll (briefly invincible)

Pointing your right controller also steers the ship. Grab triple-laser, shield and hull pickups. Survive to Wave 5 and the Ravager carrier shows up.

Getting hit costs hull — at zero hull you explode and instantly respawn with your score intact. There is no game over, ever.

No headset handy? Desktop preview works too — mouse steers, click / Space fires, Shift barrel-rolls, M mutes.
