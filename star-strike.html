STAR STRIKE

An original VR space shooter — unlimited retries, 3 weapon slots, unlimited upgrades

Open this page in your Quest Browser, then tap ENTER VR below.

You are rookie pilot Jax Vega in the interceptor Kestrel, holding the line against the Void drone swarm.

Either thumbstick steers  ·  Right trigger fires  ·  Left trigger / B = barrel roll (briefly invincible)  ·  A = Nova Bomb  ·  Click either thumbstick = recall ship to view center

Start with the base laser. Grab weapon pickups — Triple Laser, Seeker Missiles, Plasma Cannon, Rail Beam, Nova Bomb — up to 3 equipped at once (a 4th replaces your oldest weapon). Upgrade pickups — Hull Plating, Shield Cells, Engine Speed, Weapon Power — stack with no maximum. Each cleared wave also grants a bonus upgrade.

Difficulty climbs as you play: every cleared wave makes enemies faster, more numerous, and quicker to fire — and every 10,000 points raises your tier, unlocking elite drones, asteroid storms, and bomber squadrons. Boss waves (every 5th) add a new Ravager attack pattern each time.

Getting hit costs hull — at zero hull you explode and instantly respawn with your score, weapons and upgrades intact. There is no game over, ever.

No headset handy? Desktop preview works too — mouse steers, click / Space fires, Shift barrel-rolls, N = Nova Bomb, M mutes, R = recall ship.
