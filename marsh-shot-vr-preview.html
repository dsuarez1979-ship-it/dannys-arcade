MARSH SHOT VR

Cinematic prototype — not the live version. ACES lighting, richer marsh, in-VR pause + mute.

A bright day over the marsh. Wild birds cross the sky — take your shot.

VR: aim with your right controller, pull the trigger to shoot. B pauses, A toggles sound, grip restarts while paused.
Desktop: move the mouse to aim, click to shoot. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts while paused.
Farther and faster birds are worth more points. Rounds of 10 birds — every round gets faster.
No lives, no fail state — play as long as you like, rounds keep coming.
