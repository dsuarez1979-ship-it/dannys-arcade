MARSH SHOT VR

Round 3 prototype — two modes: Duck Hunt with true 3D flight paths + Clay Shooting. Red reticle, shotguns in both hands, either trigger shoots.

A bright day over the marsh. Pick your hunt — then take your shot.

Ducks fly real 3D paths — crossing, diagonal, toward you, and away into the distance. Rounds of 10 birds.

VR: aim with either controller, pull its trigger to shoot. B pauses, A toggles sound, grip restarts while paused. While paused, aim at a mode on the pause panel + trigger to switch modes.
Desktop: move the mouse to aim, click to shoot. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts while paused.
Farther and faster birds are worth more points — long shots (30 m+) earn a bonus. Rounds of 10 — every round gets faster.
Clay Shooting: orange discs launch from the hide — singles and doubles. Break them mid-air; rounds of 10 targets, with accuracy stats.
No lives, no fail state — play as long as you like, rounds keep coming.

Models: “Mallard duck” & “Duck” (Canada goose) by Poly by Google, CC-BY 3.0; dog & shotgun by Quaternius, CC0 — via Poly Pizza. Add ?debug to the URL for the aim + performance diagnostics.
