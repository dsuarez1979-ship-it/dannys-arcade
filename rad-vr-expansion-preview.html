Sunset Highway Racer · World Expansion

Pick a location, then tap ENTER VR below. Four worlds, one highway.

Journey drives all four locations in one continuous road trip — no menu stops. Scenic drive is traffic-free with no tier pressure.

Session performance (frame times + valve state) is logged locally on this device only, to help tune the game. No personal data, never sent anywhere.

Open this page in your Quest Browser, then tap ENTER VR. No headset handy? Desktop preview works too — arrow keys / WASD drive, M mutes.

Thumbstick steers  ·  Right trigger = gas  ·  Left trigger = brake

Two-way highway: cross the median to face oncoming traffic — wrong-way near misses score double and build a bonus. Brake for hairpins: tight curves push you wide.

Neon road pickups grant weapon items — Turbo Boost, Horn Blast, Oil Slick — hold up to 3 at once (a 4th replaces the oldest) — plus stacking upgrades (Engine, Grip, Armor) with no cap. No lives, no game over, ever.

Difficulty climbs as you drive: every 1,500 m raises the SPEED TIER (traffic +6% faster, +1 car — oncoming traffic joins at Tier 3) and every 5,000 points triggers a 30-second RUSH with doubled bonus points. Each location has curve challenges — hold the target speed through the marked zone for bonus points.

XR: left squeeze cycles held items · right squeeze fires selected · Desktop: B boost, H horn, O oil (only if held).

In game: press A on the right controller (or T on desktop) to cycle Day → Sunset → Night.

In VR: B pauses (menu: resume · location · mute · comfort vignette · restart) · Y mutes. Desktop: P pauses, M mutes.
