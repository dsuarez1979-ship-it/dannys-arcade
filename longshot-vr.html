LONGSHOT

An original VR mountain sniper range — endless targets, no fail state, ever

You hold a fixed firing position on a golden-hour mountain range. Steel plates at 100 / 300 / 600 yards, sliding movers, pop-ups behind the berms, and drifting balloons — farther targets score more, and the wind pushes long shots sideways, so read the flags and compensate.

Pick your rifle (or cycle in-game with B):

VR: aim with the right controller  ·  Right trigger fires  ·  Hold squeeze or A = scope (aim snaps to scope center)  ·  guns are always loaded — no reloading, ever  ·  B cycles rifles

Desktop: mouse aims  ·  click fires  ·  hold right-click or Shift = scope  ·  always loaded, no reloading  ·  1–4 switch rifles  ·  M mutes

Targets respawn endlessly and your score only ever climbs. There is no fail screen and no end — shoot as long as you like.
