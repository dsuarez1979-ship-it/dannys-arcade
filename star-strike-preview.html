About · controls · how to play

Story: Commander Nova Vega leads the last fighter wing of the United Earth Fleet. You are the wing's ace pilot — the Meridian's escort, flying a Kestrel-class strike fighter.

VR controls: Left stick thrust/strafe · Right stick yaw/pitch · Right trigger fire · Grip secondary weapon (remappable) · Left trigger barrel roll · B/Y brake · A/X cycle target · Stick click recenter heading · Hold stick click pause. Pause: A/X toggles sound, grip restarts the run, thrust selects a setting and yaw changes it.

Desktop: W/S thrust · A/D strafe · Mouse yaw/pitch · Click/Space fire · Q secondary · Shift barrel roll · B brake · Tab cycle target · N Nova · M mute · P pause · R recenter (restart while paused).

Weapons: Pulse Lasers, Scatter Cannon, Nova Torpedoes — plus Nova missiles from the Kestrel on demand. Three upgrade tiers: damage, fire rate, and heat (reheat + cooling).

Difficulty: waves escalate enemy speed and numbers; every 5th wave the Ravager carrier attacks (each appearance adds a pattern). Your hull refills over time, and there is no game over — the run always continues.

Respawn: If you fly far off course, press the RECENTER button (or the VR heading reset) and the game restores you to the fight — you will never get stranded. Gate beacons stay visible as your waypoints.

Session performance (frame times + valve level) is logged locally on this device only — nothing is sent anywhere.
