NEON INVADERS VR

Space Invaders in VR — unlimited ships & upgrades, 1 weapon at a time, Iron Man hand cannons

Open this page in your Quest Browser, then tap ENTER VR below.

Thumbstick slides the cannon  ·  Point the right controller to aim  ·  Trigger fires

Invaders surround you from every angle in deep space. Blast them all — if a flyer reaches you, or a bomb finds you, you lose a ship… and get another. Forever. There is no game over.

You start with the base bolt only. Floating pickups — shoot them to collect: Spread Shot, Twin Laser, Rail Beam, Seeker Missiles, Spartan Laser, Fire Rate, Shield Layers, Cannon Power, Move Speed. One weapon at a time — a new weapon replaces the one you have. Upgrades stack forever, no caps. Flyers lunge at you about every 2 seconds, and every wave gets harder. Asteroids drift around you and shooting stars cross the sky — pure scenery.

No headset handy? Desktop preview works too — mouse aims, A/D or mouse slides the cannon, click / Space fires, M mutes.
