Sunset Highway Racer

Open this page in your Quest Browser, then tap ENTER VR below.

Thumbstick steers  ·  Right trigger = gas  ·  Left trigger = brake

Two-way highway: cross the median to face oncoming traffic — wrong-way near misses score double and build a bonus.

No headset handy? Desktop preview works too — arrow keys / WASD drive, M mutes.

NEW: neon road pickups grant weapon items — Turbo Boost, Horn Blast, Oil Slick — hold up to 3 at once (a 4th replaces the oldest) — plus stacking upgrades (Engine, Grip, Armor) with no cap. No lives, no game over, ever.

Difficulty climbs as you drive: every 1,500 m raises the SPEED TIER (traffic +6% faster, +1 car — oncoming traffic joins at Tier 3) and every 5,000 points triggers a 30-second RUSH with doubled bonus points.

XR: left squeeze cycles held items · right squeeze fires selected · Desktop: B boost, H horn, O oil (only if held).

In game: press A on the right controller (or T on desktop) to cycle Day → Sunset → Night.
