Controls & comfort

Quest 2: left stick steers, right trigger accelerates, left trigger brakes. Right stick trims speed and snap-turns. B/Y pauses. A/X recenters.

Desktop: W/S speed, A/D bank, arrows pitch/turn, drag to look. Space launches; P pauses; M mutes.

Flight and sound controls are in the in-world menu. Stop if you feel uncomfortable. Performance logs stay on your device — tap EXPORT PERF LOG above to download the session log.
