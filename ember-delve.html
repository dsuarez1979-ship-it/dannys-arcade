EMBER DELVE

Kael the Ember Knight enters the Sunken Halls of Cindermaw. Five chambers. Switch puzzles, locked doors, ember-crystals, monsters — and something big asleep at the bottom.

Quest: left stick to walk · right stick to turn · right trigger to swing your sword · left trigger to block.
Desktop: WASD to walk · mouse to look (click to lock) · click to swing · right mouse to block.

Fall in battle and you wake at the chamber door — retries are unlimited. There is no way to lose this delve for good.
